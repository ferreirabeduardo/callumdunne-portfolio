Tourism Northern IrelandLove It? Put It On The Map

+2 million people in Northern Ireland know it better than any guidebook does. So, we gave them somewhere to prove it.

Together with Tourism Northern Ireland, we built something genuinely new: an interactive map where locals, not the tourist board, decide what's worth visiting in the country. The idea only works if people actually use it. A map with nothing pinned to it is just an empty page with Northern Ireland's outline on it, so the real job wasn't launching the concept, it was making sure it filled up with things worth travelling to.

That's where I came in. I ran the social push encouraging people to tag their own favourite spots with #MyGiantAdventure, the beach only locals go to, the restaurant that hasn't blown up on TikTok yet, the forest walk you and your family grew up on. Alongside that, I reached out directly to local creators across Northern Ireland and briefed them to go find and shoot the corners of the country they felt deserved more attention, the places that don't show up on a typical Giant's Causeway itinerary.

Once the content came in, I curated it onto the map itself, so what launched wasn't a blank interactive tool waiting for content, it was one that already had a country's worth of recommendations sitting on it from day one.

Insights showed that travellers were already looking for something just like this. Amex's 2023 Global Travel Trends Report found 89% of global travellers now want local hidden gems over the well-known spots.

The map didn't have to convince anyone hidden gems mattered. It just needed enough of them on it to encourage them to book the trip.
