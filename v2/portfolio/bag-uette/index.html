CentraChicken Fillet Bag-uette

An April Fools' bag for a chicken fillet roll. The internet still asked where to buy one.

Every April Fools' joke a brand runs is competing with every other brand's April Fools' joke on the same day, all fighting for the same five minutes of someone's scroll. Centra's chicken fillet roll is one of the most-loved, most-argued-about foods in Ireland, their delis are widely considered where the whole thing started, so a generic prank wasn't going to cut it. It had to be something only Centra could plausibly claim.

I came up with the idea: a “Chicken Fillet Bag-uette,” a purpose-built carry bag for your chicken fillet roll, shaped like the roll itself, designed to keep it (and you) fresh on the go. The wordplay did a lot of the work on its own, but the detail I liked most was the launch date: the 31st of April, a date that doesn't exist on any calendar. If you clocked it, you were in on the joke immediately. If you didn't, you spent a minute genuinely confused about why Centra was launching a bag for a sandwich, which is exactly the reaction you want from an April Fools' post.

I helped direct the shoot alongside BOLT at TBWA\Ireland, treating the whole thing with the same seriousness as a real product launch: a proper fashion-led shoot inspired by brands including ASOS and Zara, proper copy, nothing winking at the camera until the date gave it away.

It travelled fast. The Twitter/X posts alone organically hit +1M views within days, and the reaction across Facebook and Instagram was just as strong, with fans asking where to buy one, people tagging their chicken fillet roll loving friends, the exact response you want from a joke this specific to one country's food culture. The best compliment a faux product launch can get is people being disappointed it isn't real, and that's exactly what happened.

The chicken fillet roll didn't need reinventing. It just needed Centra to say the quiet part out loud: people already carry this thing around in any way they can to protect it from the harsh Irish elements. So, we simply played into the reality and gave it luggage.
